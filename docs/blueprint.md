# **App Name**: Aroma Affaires Atelier

## Core Features:

- Luxury Product Showcase: An elegantly designed interface to showcase premium perfumes with high-quality imagery, detailed descriptions, and various sizes (variants) for each product.
- Variant Selection & Add-ons: Allow customers to select specific perfume variants (e.g., size) and personalize their order with luxurious add-ons such as gift wrapping and custom messages.
- Intuitive Guest Cart & Checkout: Enable seamless guest shopping with a dynamic cart for managing items and a streamlined checkout flow featuring Cash on Delivery (COD) and address submission without requiring an account.
- Secure Admin Product Control: A protected administrative dashboard to manage the entire product catalog, including creating, updating, and deleting perfumes, their variants, images, and categories.
- Admin Order Management System: A comprehensive interface for administrators to view, filter, and update the status of customer orders (e.g., pending, confirmed, shipped, delivered) with detailed order information.
- Scalable RESTful API: A robust Next.js API layer providing well-defined endpoints for efficient management and retrieval of product, variant, category, and order data, designed for decoupled frontend/backend interaction.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: Elegant Gold (#EAB446). This luminous hue signifies luxury and exclusivity, contrasting beautifully against the dark background.
- Background Color: Deep Charcoal (#26221A). A warm, desaturated dark tone that provides a sophisticated and immersive backdrop, making the products and gold accents truly stand out.
- Accent Color: Soft Ivory (#E5DCD6). A delicate and refined neutral beige for secondary text, subtle highlights, and interactive elements, adding warmth and approachability to the luxury aesthetic.
- Headline Font: 'Playfair', a modern serif font with high contrast and an elegant, fashionable appeal, perfect for conveying luxury. Body Font: 'PT Sans', a clean, humanist sans-serif for optimal readability in product descriptions and detailed content.
- Utilize minimalist, sophisticated line-art icons that complement the high-end aesthetic. Icons should be refined, abstract where possible, and communicate function without clutter.
- Employ a spacious, grid-based layout with generous use of whitespace. Prioritize full-width sections for hero imagery and product displays, ensuring a responsive and visually clean experience across all devices with a focus on product photography.
- Implement subtle, smooth transitions and micro-interactions. Delicate fade-ins, gentle lifts or subtle shifts on hover, and soft animations for form elements will enhance the immersive and elegant user experience without being distracting.
